About SAUrted

Making exam prep a little less chaotic.

What is SAUrted?

SAUrted is a student-driven platform that provides easy access to previous year question papers for B.Tech CSE students at South Asian University. Instead of searching through scattered sources, everything is organized in one place.

Why does it exist?

Many students struggle to find reliable study materials, especially when they are new to the university. Academic resources often exist but are difficult to access. SAUrted aims to simplify this by bringing everything together in a structured and easy-to-use format.

How does it work?

  • Select your year and semester
  • Choose your subject
  • Access previous year papers instantly
